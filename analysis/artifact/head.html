Brownlow Vote Tracker

2026 InTheGame model, live through the count: votes as they are read out against what the model expected, the season re-simulated continuously over only the matches still unread, and the Sportsbet lines the model rates as value.

Each round cell shows what the player actually polled (large, shaded) over what the model expected for that match (small). A dot means that match has not been read out yet; blank means a bye or a game not played. Votes = banked so far, a fact; Expected = what the model expected that player to poll over those same matches, so Votes minus Expected is the "v model" column. Games left and Exp left are the matches still to be read for that player and the votes the model expects from them, so Votes plus Exp left is roughly Proj. Clicking a round heading sorts on what the model expected in that round, not on what was polled, which is what you want for a round still to come. Proj = mean final total once the unread matches are re-simulated and added to those banked votes, and the count of simulations above says how many have gone into the numbers so far; 10-90 is the 10th to 90th percentile of that final total; Pre-count is what the model projected before a single vote was read. Win % is the chance of an outright win from here, higher is better; Δ Win % is the move since before the count; "v model" is votes polled minus votes expected over the rounds read so far, so positive means running hot. The page keeps re-running counts in the background so the numbers sharpen while you watch, and reloads every two and a half minutes to pick up the votes read out since.

Click a column header to sort. Hover a round cell for opponent and the chance of the 3 votes. Blank cells are byes or games not played. xV = expected votes; Sim = mean total across 5,000 simulated counts (± one standard deviation).
